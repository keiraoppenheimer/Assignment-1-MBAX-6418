Why exact agreement is low. NRC frequently marks a review with several tied emotions (e.g. anticipation;joy;surprise) and its primary label is chosen by a fixed deterministic tie-break (first in canonical order = often anticipation). The LLM instead names a single, more salient emotion (usually joy for positive gift-card reviews). So exact string equality understates agreement: when we accept the LLM's emotion as "agreeing" if it is any of the NRC tied candidates, agreement rises to 75.00%.

Reconciling the 100 reviews: 15 NRC NO_MATCH + 1 LLM NO_EMOTION + 84 both-concrete = 100.
